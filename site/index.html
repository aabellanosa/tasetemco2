Process under Membership means the role can encode member applications. Approve means the role can convert approved applications into active members. System Administrator and Membership Officer can create member applications, while System Administrator approves them. Teller / Cashier records initial payments and Accountant / Bookkeeper posts reviewed teller batches. Member imports map and validate Excel or CSV rows before approved records become active member profiles. Imported profiles begin with zero financial balances. Opening balance imports are reviewed separately so confirmed share capital and savings can be applied with source references and audit evidence. The Ledger workspace is organized into role-aware tabs for Batch Review, Opening Balances, Batch History, and Posted Entries so users open one ledger work area at a time. Opening balance rows with issues remain unapplied for correction. Finalized rows retain actor, timing, source, and cutover evidence and cannot run twice. Finalization creates one balanced journal: debit Opening Balance Clearing, credit Share Capital, and credit Savings Deposits Payable. The journal is linked to the batch and appears in Posted Entries and reports. Member Statements show finalized opening balances with their batch, cutover date, source reference, and journal number. Member Subsidiary Ledger separates opening share capital and savings from normal transaction movements. The Loans workspace provides reusable Regular, Emergency, and Small Business loan templates. Admin maintains amount and term limits, rates, fees, penalties, status, and accounting mappings. Manager, Loan Officer, Credit Committee / Approver, Teller / Cashier, and Auditor have read-only product access. The Loan Officer creates and edits Draft applications, with active-member checks and product amount and term limits enforced. Product rates, methods, fees, penalties, and accounting mappings are copied into the application as an audit snapshot. Submitting makes the record read-only and visible to Admin, Manager, Credit Committee / Approver, and Auditor. Loan Credit Review lets the Credit Committee / Approver record assessment notes, recommended amount and term, decision date, and remarks before choosing Approved, Rejected, or Returned. Returned applications go back to the originating Loan Officer for revision and resubmission. Approval cannot exceed the member's request, and decided applications are immutable. The originating Loan Officer selects the first payment date and previews the Flat Interest schedule, processing fee, net proceeds, total payable, and maturity, then saves it once as For Release. Whole-peso rounding differences go to the final installment. Teller / Cashier verifies the computed amounts, enters a unique voucher and release date, and confirms cash equal to net proceeds. The release is immutable, linked to the Open teller batch, and counted as cash-out. Bookkeeper posts the Reviewed batch: debit Loans Receivable for principal, credit Cash for net proceeds, and credit Processing Fee Income for the fee. The release and loan become Posted and retain their linked journal number. Teller Cash Funding establishes custody before payouts. Bookkeeper prepares funding from an identified source, Manager approves it, and the assigned Teller acknowledges it into the Open batch. Expected ending cash is Opening Funding plus receipts minus payouts, and batch details retain all three control actors. Bookkeeper sees pending loan-release demand, available teller cash, and shortage. Teller cannot release a loan whose net proceeds exceed acknowledged funding plus current-batch receipts less existing payouts. Prepared or Approved funding counts only after Teller acknowledgment. When Bookkeeper posts the Reviewed batch, the system debits Cash on Hand and credits the funding source, normally Cash in Bank, while posting the loan release in the same action. Funding keeps its Acknowledged custody status and displays its linked journal. Teller can collect the earliest unpaid installment of a posted loan. The scheduled principal, interest, and total cannot be edited; early payment is allowed after release, while partial and excess payments are deferred. The collection enters the Open teller batch as cash-in. Teller submits cash count from Members and Bookkeeper reviews and posts debit Cash on Hand, credit Loans Receivable, and credit Interest Income. Acknowledgment opens the Releases tab. The Release button requires a computed loan marked For Release and an Open batch; it does not depend on another teller transaction. Initial member payment is one-time only; later member activity uses the normal savings deposit, savings withdrawal, or share capital contribution workflows. Cash-in OR/reference numbers cannot be reused across initial payments, share capital contributions, savings deposits, and loan collections. Withdrawal voucher/reference numbers cannot be reused across withdrawals. Teller / Cashier can submit actual cash counted for the unposted batch, while Accountant / Bookkeeper reviews expected cash, actual cash, and variance before posting. The implemented batch status flow is Open to Submitted to Reviewed to Closed, then the next teller batch opens for new activity. Posting is allowed only after review. Bookkeeper posts the reviewed batch in one action, while the system keeps source-level journal entries for audit traceability. Non-zero variance requires a Bookkeeper variance note before review. Official close records closed by, closed time, and optional closing note. Teller Batch History remains available as read-only evidence after closing, and each row can be opened to inspect cash count evidence, transactions, and linked journals. Reports now use a selector layout so users open one report at a time. Daily Cash Position summarizes teller batch cash evidence in Reports. Member Subsidiary Ledger summarizes member share capital and savings balances in Reports. Control Account Reconciliation compares recorded subsidiary activity to general ledger controls. Trial Balance summarizes posted GL debit and credit totals and flags whether the ledger is balanced. Statement of Financial Condition presents assets, liabilities, and equity from posted ledger balances. The Members workflow refreshes automatically, with a manual Refresh button available when users want the latest state immediately. The Members screen is now organized into role-aware tabs for Applications, Imports, Teller Transactions, Member Directory, and Transaction History.

Operating flow

Core Cooperative Workflows

Member Application

  1. Membership Officer encodes application.
  2. Required Initial Share Capital is reviewed.
  3. Application is submitted for approval.
  4. System Administrator approves membership.
  5. Teller collects initial share capital, membership fee, and savings.
  6. Admin or Membership Officer maintains member profile master data.
  7. Admin or Membership Officer can preview mapped Excel/CSV member imports.
  8. Admin or Membership Officer can stage mapped import rows for review.
  9. System blocks duplicate initial member payments for the same member.
  10. System blocks reused cash-in OR/reference numbers.
  11. Transaction remains in teller batch until accounting posting.

Teller Transaction

  1. Teller selects active member account.
  2. Member statement shows share capital, savings, and posting status.
  3. Teller chooses one transaction type in the workspace.
  4. Share capital contribution is available after onboarding.
  5. Selected transaction is encoded and validated.
  6. System blocks reused OR or voucher reference numbers.
  7. Receipt is issued.
  8. Cash in, cash out, net cash, and transaction counts are summarized.
  9. Teller submits actual cash counted and system computes variance.
  10. Bookkeeper records a variance note when the cash count has non-zero variance.
  11. Bookkeeper marks the submitted teller batch as reviewed.
  12. System allows posting only after the batch is reviewed.
  13. Bookkeeper posts the reviewed teller batch in one action.
  14. Bookkeeper confirms official close after its transactions are posted.
  15. System keeps read-only batch history for audit review.
  16. Batch history rows can be opened for cash count, transaction, and journal details.
  17. Reports summarize daily cash position from teller batch evidence.
  18. Reports summarize member subsidiary balances for share capital and savings.
  19. Reports compare subsidiary activity against GL control accounts.
  20. Transaction stays in teller batch until posting.

Loan Application

  1. Loan Officer selects an active member and active loan product.
  2. System validates requested principal and term against product limits.
  3. Loan Officer saves and may edit their own Draft.
  4. Loan Officer submits the Draft, making it read-only.
  5. Credit Committee / Approver records assessment notes and a decision.
  6. Returned applications go back to Draft for Loan Officer revision.
  7. Approved and Rejected applications retain immutable decision evidence.
  8. Loan Officer previews and saves the approved amortization schedule.
  9. Saved computations move to For Release without moving cash.
  10. Bookkeeper prepares Teller funding and Manager approves it.
  11. Assigned Teller acknowledges funding into the Open batch.
  12. Teller confirms the unique release voucher and exact net cash proceeds.
  13. Released proceeds enter the Open teller batch as cash-out evidence.
  14. Bookkeeper reviews the batch and records any required variance note.
  15. Posting creates the balanced loan release journal and linked evidence.

Ledger Posting

  1. Teller payment remains in Teller Batch status.
  2. Bookkeeper reviews the supporting transaction.
  3. Balanced entries are posted to Cash, receivables, capital, income, and deposit controls.
  4. Opening balance imports are previewed separately before any cutover balance write exists.
  5. Reports are generated from posted ledger entries.

Data protection

Demonstration Data Safety

The System Administrator can download a backup and restore the demonstration records when testing input needs to be cleared. Reset requires typed confirmation and preserves a pre-reset backup for recovery.

Database maintenance and deployment steps are handled by the implementation team. Staff users work through the application screens and do not need access to database tools or hosting settings.

The System Administrator can create demonstration staff users, assign roles and default screens, and deactivate non-admin accounts. Demonstration accounts use a shared temporary password until individual password management is introduced. The Auditor / Compliance Officer can review usernames, roles, default screens, and account status through a read-only User / Security directory without password, maintenance, backup, or reset access.

Accounting output

Ledger-Driven Reports

TASETEMCO treats the general ledger as the source of financial statements. Savings, share capital, and loans should reconcile to their subsidiary ledgers.
