# TASETEMCO Member Portal Help

The Member Portal gives you a read-only view of your cooperative account. It is separate from the staff system.

## First login

1. Ask an authorized TASETEMCO staff member for your username and temporary password.
2. Select **Member Portal Login** on the TASETEMCO login page.
3. Enter the issued credentials.
4. Create a new password when prompted. Use at least 10 characters, including an uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character.

The temporary password works only until you replace it. Do not share either password.

## Account overview

The overview shows your member number and name, classification, membership status and date, CBU, regular and secured savings, system and previous loans, and outstanding cost-center dues. The displayed **as of** time tells you when the view was generated.

The portal is strictly read-only. It does not display government or other ID numbers, beneficiaries, or tools for changing cooperative records.

## Overdue loans and penalties

The Loans section shows an overdue amount and penalty amount separately. An unpaid scheduled loan amount receives a 2% penalty after a seven-calendar-day grace period. If you paid part of the scheduled amount, the 2% is calculated only on the portion that remained unpaid when the penalty was assessed.

The penalty is carried into collection with the next billing period and remains visible separately from principal and interest. Please contact the TASETEMCO office if a payment or displayed balance needs review; members cannot edit loan or payment records through the portal.

## Trouble signing in

An authorized System Administrator, General Manager, or Membership Officer can reset your password, unlock your account, or re-enable it. A reset issues a new temporary password and requires another password change. For your protection, staff cannot retrieve your existing password.
